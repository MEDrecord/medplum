# HealthTalk Brand Style Guide

> AI Based Clinical Reporting

## Brand Identity

### Brand Archetype: The Sage

HealthTalk embodies "The Sage" - a trusted, intelligent guide in mental healthcare.
Values knowledge, wisdom, and truth. Helps professionals understand complex patient data.

### Brand Voice

Clear, intelligent, and supportive. We speak directly and professionally, offering expert
guidance and practical insights. Our tone is always trustworthy, empathetic, and focused
on empowering professionals to work more efficiently and effectively.

### Brand Promise

HealthTalk promises to give mental health professionals back their valuable time by
providing secure, insightful, and precise AI-powered documentation, allowing them to
focus more on patient care.

### Brand Personality

Knowledgeable, reliable, and forward-thinking. We are an intelligent guide committed to
empowering professionals with innovative, secure, and user-friendly technology.

### Tagline

"AI BASED CLINICAL REPORTING"

### Typography

#### Primary Font: Outfit

Modern, clean sans-serif that conveys innovation and precision.

```css
font-family: 'Outfit', sans-serif;
```

| Element | Size | Weight |
|---------|------|--------|
| H1 | 36-48pt | 700 |
| H2 | 24-30pt | 600 |
| H3 | 20-24pt | 600 |
| Body | 16-18pt | 400 |

#### Secondary Font: Inter

Excellent legibility on screens, ideal for body text.

```css
font-family: 'Inter', sans-serif;
```

| Element | Size | Weight |
|---------|------|--------|
| Body | 16-18pt | 400 |
| Small | 14pt | 400 |
| Caption | 12pt | 500 |

## Target Audience

Mental healthcare professionals (psychologists, therapists, psychiatrists) aged 30-60,
seeking to reduce administrative burden and improve patient care through AI-powered
documentation tools.

## Competitive Positioning

HealthTalk is the authoritative source for mental health-specific AI insights.
Not just about efficiency - it's about informed efficiency with deep clinical understanding.
